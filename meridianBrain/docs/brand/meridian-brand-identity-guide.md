## About Meridian

Meridian is the Collective Brain for Platform Engineering. What started as Dream Copilot — an autonomous ticket workflow built by and for the CRM (Salesforce) team — has evolved into the shared memory, context engine, and compounding intelligence for every domain across Platform Engineering: CRM, Contact Center, Marketing Automation, Integration, Business Apps, SIS, HCM, and Portal.

Meridian eliminates the knowledge silos, inconsistent processes, and cross-team blind spots that slow Platform Engineering down. It captures institutional knowledge before it walks out the door, maps cross-domain dependencies so nothing falls through the cracks, and automates the ticket lifecycle from intake to resolution.

Today, Meridian powers autonomous ticket workflows with 40+ AI prompts, 8 CLI tools, and a phased ticket lifecycle that keeps work moving without babysitting. The CRM domain is live, Contact Center is next, and Marketing Automation follows. It's not just a tool — it's how Platform Engineering thinks, works, and ships together.

Meridian is a GitHub repository. Not a web application, not a SaaS product, not a database. It is a structured repository that engineers clone to their local machine and interact with through their IDE using GitHub Copilot Enterprise. Everything — prompts, agents, skills, CLI tools, configuration, knowledge, templates, standards — lives in this repo as files.

## User Insight

Platform Engineering teams are busy, skilled professionals juggling multiple systems, constant context-switching, and institutional knowledge that lives in people's heads instead of shared systems. They've built successful careers managing complex integrations and enterprise platforms, but they're fighting against fragmentation every single day.

Some are CRM specialists who need to understand how Integration (MuleSoft) feeds data into their orgs — schedules, sources, and dependencies they can't easily see. Others are Integration engineers who need visibility into downstream HCM (Workday) impacts. Still others are team leads trying to onboard new members without losing months to tribal knowledge transfer.

Regardless of their platform specialty, they share the same pain: too many tools, too many handoffs, too little shared context. Meridian meets them where they are and gives them the unified view they've been stitching together manually.

## Platform Team Personas

### Navigators — 30% of users

**Motivation:** Need cross-platform visibility to coordinate work across teams
**Differentiators:** Team leads and senior engineers who work across multiple platforms daily
**Top Barriers to Adoption:** Skepticism that one tool can serve all platforms equally; existing workflows feel "good enough"

### Specialists — 25% of users

**Motivation:** Want deep tooling for their specific platform without sacrificing integration awareness
**Differentiators:** Deep expertise in one platform, limited visibility into others
**Top Barriers to Adoption:** Fear of being forced into a generic tool that doesn't respect their platform's nuances

### Inheritors — 20% of users

**Motivation:** Recently joined the team and need to get up to speed on existing systems, integrations, and tribal knowledge
**Differentiators:** Newest team members, highest learning curve, most reliant on documentation
**Top Barriers to Adoption:** Overwhelmed by the volume of information; need guided onboarding paths

### Automators — 15% of users

**Motivation:** Want to reduce manual ticket management and repetitive workflow tasks
**Differentiators:** Power users who push tools to their limits; often build custom scripts and integrations
**Top Barriers to Adoption:** Want to see that automation is reliable before trusting it with critical workflows

### Observers — 10% of users

**Motivation:** Leadership and stakeholders who need reporting, status visibility, and cross-team metrics without getting into the weeds
**Differentiators:** Don't use Meridian daily but rely on its outputs for decision-making
**Top Barriers to Adoption:** Need clear, digestible dashboards — not another tool to log into

## Imagery Philosophy

Meridian imagery focuses on the collaborative, cross-functional nature of Platform Engineering. Our visual style should convey connection, clarity, and technical credibility.

Images should depict real working moments: engineers collaborating at whiteboards, teams huddled around screens solving problems together, the satisfaction of a clean deployment. Avoid staged corporate photography. Meridian's visual identity should feel authentic — like a snapshot from the platform team's actual workday.

Favor imagery that shows diversity of people, platforms, and working styles. Show engineers in their natural habitats — whether that's a home office, an open floor plan, or a conference room with sticky notes everywhere. Movement and energy matter more than polish.

**Do:** Cross-team collaboration, real working environments, diverse teams, screen-adjacent work, whiteboard sessions, CLI/terminal aesthetics

**Don't:** Generic stock photos of people pointing at charts, overly polished corporate settings, isolated individuals staring blankly at monitors

## Typography

### Headlines (Web & App)

**Inter** — Clean, modern, highly legible at all sizes. Available via Google Fonts.

- **Inter Bold** for primary headlines (H1, H2)
- **Inter SemiBold** for subheadings (H3, H4)

### Body Copy (Web & App)

**Inter** — Same family for consistency. Regular weight for body, Medium for emphasis.

- **Inter Regular** (400) for body text
- **Inter Medium** (500) for emphasized body text

### Monospace (Code, CLI, Technical Content)

**JetBrains Mono** — Purpose-built for code readability. Used in all terminal output, code snippets, CLI tool references, and technical documentation.

- **JetBrains Mono Regular** for code blocks
- **JetBrains Mono Bold** for highlighted syntax or emphasis within code

- Headlines: **Montserrat** (Bold, SemiBold)

## The Meridian Mark

The Meridian logo represents the convergence of multiple platforms into a single, unified system. It should be treated with respect and consistency across all applications.

The logomark is a compass rose with a creative twist — symbolizing how Meridian helps engineers navigate complexity across many platforms and find their way to the context they need. The design conveys precision, technical credibility, and the confidence of the UMGC parent brand.

### Logo Concept

The name "Meridian" refers to a line of longitude — a fixed reference point that connects disparate locations on a globe. Just as a meridian provides orientation and alignment across geography, the Meridian platform provides a shared reference point across CRM, Contact Center, Marketing Automation, Integration, Business Apps, SIS, HCM, and Portal.

### Logo Variants

**Primary Logo (Preferred):** Logomark + "MERIDIAN" wordmark, horizontal lockup. Use wherever space permits.

**Stacked Logo:** Logomark centered above "MERIDIAN" wordmark. Use in square or vertical spaces.

**Wordmark Only:** "MERIDIAN" text in Inter Bold, all caps with tracked spacing. Use when the logomark has already been established in context.

**Icon Only:** Logomark without text. Use for favicons, app icons, small badges, and contexts where the brand is already established.

### Clear Space

Maintain a minimum clear space around the logo equal to the height of the "M" in the wordmark. No other text, graphics, or visual elements should intrude into this space.

### Minimum Size

- Horizontal lockup: minimum 120px wide (digital) / 1.5" wide (print)
- Icon only: minimum 24px (digital) / 0.25" (print)

### Logo Colors

The logo should appear in the following color combinations only:

| Context        | Logomark                              | Wordmark            | Background                   |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| **Primary**    | UMGC Red `#DA1A32` + Yellow `#FDBF38` | Dark Gray `#3C3C43` | White or Barely Gray         |
| **Reversed**   | White + Yellow `#FDBF38`              | White               | Dark Gray `#3C3C43` or Black |
| **Monochrome** | Black                                 | Black               | White                        |
| **Bold**       | UMGC Red `#DA1A32` + Yellow `#FDBF38` | White               | UMGC Red `#DA1A32`           |

### Logo Don'ts

- Do not stretch, rotate, or distort the logo
- Do not change the logo colors outside of approved combinations
- Do not place the logo on busy photographic backgrounds without a solid backing
- Do not add effects (drop shadows, gradients, outlines) to the logo
- Do not rearrange or separate the logomark from the wordmark in the primary lockup
- Do not recreate the logo using other fonts or symbols

When Meridian is referenced in the context of a specific platform module, sub-brand lockups may be used:

**Format:** `MERIDIAN | [DOMAIN NAME]`

Use the domain name (not the underlying technology) in formal brand materials. The platform technology may appear as a subtitle where additional context is needed.

Examples:

- MERIDIAN | CRM
- MERIDIAN | CONTACT CENTER

- MERIDIAN | BUSINESS APPS
- MERIDIAN | SIS

## Writing Guidelines

### Naming Conventions

- **The product:** "Meridian" — always capitalized, never abbreviated to "Mdn" or "M" in user-facing content
- **The tagline:** "The Collective Brain for Platform Engineering"
- **Domain references:** Always use domain names on first reference (e.g., "CRM"), with the underlying technology in parentheses if needed for clarity (e.g., "CRM (Salesforce)"). Abbreviations acceptable on subsequent references within the same document.
- **Feature references:** Use the official feature names consistently: "Ticket Lifecycle," "Dependency Map," "Knowledge Capture," "CLI Tools"

### Voice in Action

**Announcing a feature:**

> _"Meridian now maps Marketing Automation-to-CRM dependencies automatically. No more guessing what breaks downstream."_

**Error messaging:**

> _"This ticket can't move to Implementation — the linked CRM org hasn't been validated. Here's how to fix that."_

**Onboarding:**

> _"Welcome to Meridian. Let's get you connected to your platforms so you can see the full picture."_

**Release notes:**

> _"v2.4 brings cross-platform dependency alerts. When an Integration schedule changes, every connected CRM workflow gets flagged automatically."_

### What Meridian Is and Isn't

**Meridian IS:**

- The Collective Brain for Platform Engineering
- A context-aware, AI-powered workflow framework
- The shared memory that compounds intelligence with every ticket worked
- A cross-platform dependency mapper and living knowledge graph
- A GitHub repository — not a SaaS product, not a database, not a web app
- Built by engineers, for engineers, inside the IDE

**Meridian IS NOT:**

- A project management tool (it complements ADO, not replaces it)
- A monitoring/observability platform
- A code deployment tool
- A replacement for platform-specific admin consoles
- A browser-based dashboard you have to context-switch into

## Accessibility

Meridian is committed to WCAG 2.1 AA compliance across all interfaces. All color combinations used for text must meet minimum contrast ratios, and all interactive elements must be keyboard-navigable.

All brand color pairings listed in this guide have been validated for accessible contrast. When creating new materials, verify contrast ratios using the WebAIM Contrast Checker or equivalent tool.
