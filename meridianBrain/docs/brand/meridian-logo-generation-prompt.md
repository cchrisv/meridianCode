Design a logo for **MERIDIAN — The Collective Brain for Platform Engineering**.

**About the parent brand:** Meridian is an internal product built at the University of Maryland Global Campus (UMGC), one of the largest public universities in the United States. UMGC's brand identity is bold and confident — anchored by a rich red, warm gold/yellow, and black. Their existing logo features a stylized globe with curved, flowing bands in red, gold, and black. Meridian should feel like it belongs in this brand family — a sibling, not a stranger.

**About the app:** Meridian is an AI-powered knowledge and workflow system used by enterprise platform engineering teams. It connects siloed technology teams (CRM, Marketing Automation, Contact Center, Integration, etc.) into one shared intelligence layer. It remembers institutional knowledge that would otherwise be lost when people leave, maps hidden dependencies between systems, and gets smarter with every ticket worked. Think of it as the collective memory and navigation system for an entire engineering organization.

**Logo direction:** The logomark should be a **compass rose — with a fun, creative twist**. Surprise me. Have fun with it. The compass represents how Meridian helps engineers navigate complexity across many platforms and find their way to the context they need.

**Colors — use only these (no exceptions):**

- Red #DA1A32 (primary)
- Gold #FDBF38 (accent)
- Black #000000
- White #FFFFFF
- No blue. No purple. No green.

**Style:** Flat, geometric, professional. No gradients, no 3D. Must scale from favicon (24px) to presentation slide. Enterprise-grade — confident, not cutesy.

**Wordmark:** "MERIDIAN" in a clean, bold sans-serif (like Inter or similar), all caps, generous letter spacing. Dark gray #3C3C43 on light backgrounds, white on dark.

Generate: horizontal lockup (icon + wordmark), stacked version, icon only, and a reversed version on dark gray background.
